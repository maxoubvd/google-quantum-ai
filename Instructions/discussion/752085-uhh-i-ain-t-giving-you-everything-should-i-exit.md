**María Cruz** — **Kaggle staff** · 2026-10-27 21:51 UTC · 1 votes

Hi @jasonchewning (and @jday96314, @greysnow),
jumping in here to clarify how the open-source requirements and leaderboard eligibility work under the Competition Rules:

1. **Apache 2.0 open-source delivery applies only if you accept a prize:** The Winner License and source-code delivery obligations (delivering the code and methodology used to generate your circuits under Apache 2.0) apply **only to prize winners as a condition of receiving a prize** after the competition closes on January 20, 2027. If a team finishes in a prize position and chooses to decline the prize, they are not required to open-source their solution under the Winner License, and **compliant teams are not removed from the leaderboard simply for declining a prize.**
2. **Rule on pre-existing proprietary software/tools:** Please keep in mind the distinction between code you write for the competition and pre-existing proprietary software. While code you develop for the challenge does not have to be open-sourced unless you accept a prize, **any external tools or software used to generate a submission must comply with the External Data & Tools rules (meaning they must be publicly and reasonably accessible to all participants).** Submissions generated using private, closed-source proprietary software that other participants cannot access are not eligible for the leaderboard.

Please let us know if you have any other questions!

Best,

María
