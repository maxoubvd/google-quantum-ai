# Free introductory guide to FTQC circuit optimization for this competition

> Kaggle discussion topic: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/google-verifiable-quantum-circuit-compilation/discussion/754970 — copied 2026-11-15 (raw Markdown, unedited).

**snuffkin** · 2026-11-02 14:04 UTC · 14 votes

I wrote a free introductory guide for this competition, aimed at programmers who are new to quantum computing.

It covers the basics of quantum circuits, reversible computing, phase operations, measurement-based uncomputation, and FTQC cost models.

The guide focuses on general techniques and background knowledge, rather than the advanced optimization techniques used by top teams on the leaderboard.

The book is written in Japanese, but feel free to use AI translation tools to read it in your preferred language.

PDF: https://zenodo.org/records/23875845

I hope it helps!
