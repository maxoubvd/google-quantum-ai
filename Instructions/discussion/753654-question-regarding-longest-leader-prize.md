# Question regarding longest leader prize

> Kaggle discussion topic: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/google-verifiable-quantum-circuit-compilation/discussion/753654 — copied 2026-11-15 (raw Markdown, unedited).

**Geremie Yeo** · 2026-10-28 21:46 UTC · 8 votes

From the description

```
Longest Leader Prize
Awarded to the team whose notebook(s) generates the best scoring submission on the leaderboard for the longest period of time between the competition start date and January 4th, 2027. The notebook(s) and any datasets used must be made publicly available at latest at January 4th, 2027 11:59 PM UTC and kept public until the end of the competition. In the event that a team has multiple public notebooks, each achieving the top leaderboard position at different times, those durations will be combined.
```

That's only 16 days before the competition ends. If a top scoring notebook was released at that time won't it disrupt the leaderboard massively with only just 2 weeks remaining in the competition? I would suppose top teams won't be open sourcing their solution so late into the competition. Then it kinda defeats the purpose.

My suggestion would be to just to extend the longest leader prize to span `competition start date to January 20th, 2027 (the competition end date)` like in NeuroGolf.
