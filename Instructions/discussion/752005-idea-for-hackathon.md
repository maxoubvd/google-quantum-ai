# Idea for hackathon

> Kaggle discussion topic: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/google-verifiable-quantum-circuit-compilation/discussion/752005 — copied 2026-11-15 (raw Markdown, unedited).

**hengck23** · 2026-10-23 12:15 UTC · 11 votes

This is my first time learning about quantum computing, and ChatGPT and Claude have helped me a lot.

I think that if there is another Kaggle Gemini app-building competition in the future, one interesting idea would be to create an interactive warm-up app that helps Kagglers quickly learn the domain knowledge required for a competition.

Such an app could include:
- Interactive animations to explain key concepts intuitively.
- Hands-on exercises to experiment with new ideas and test our understanding.
- Simple code examples that gradually introduce more advanced concepts.

For this particular quantum computing competition, I think the following topics would be especially useful:

(1) Fundamentals of quantum computing
- Qubits, quantum states, and Dirac notation (e.g., (|0\rangle), (|1\rangle), and (|x\rangle)).
- Quantum gates and circuits (X, H, CNOT, Toffoli, etc.).
- Reversible computation and how classical algorithms can be translated into quantum circuits.
- Ancilla qubits, uncomputation, and resource optimization.- ... more ...

 
(2) Benchmark 2  
- Reed–Solomon codes and finite-field arithmetic.  
- Polynomial representations and arithmetic over finite fields.  
- Modular arithmetic and efficient circuit construction.  
- Error correction and coding theory.
 ....
