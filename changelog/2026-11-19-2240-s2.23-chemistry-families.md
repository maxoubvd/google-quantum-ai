## What was done

A design agent priced seven chemistry families on paper with the scorer's own formulas, red-teamed each against
the chemistry nulls (ours, the 152 community rows, the 19 Nov public reports), and measured one new quantity: the
sum of per-plane core ranks of the loaded bit-planes (`tools/core_rank.py`, ~1 s), a proxy for the loaders'
product count.

## Why

After S2.18 (invsqrt) and S2.21 (dqi) found no family at the leaders' scale, chemistry was the last place where a
−20% to −35% family might exist.

## Results and analysis

- **No −20% to −35% chemistry family exists under this scorer, even on paper.** Q is pinned at 427 for every
  viable shape; λ_eff is 0.28% above its hard floor; the largest prize in the cost model (a smaller inner QROAM block,
  Q 427 → 276) is only chosen by the scorer at brot ≤ 3 or R·C·B < 2,432, out of reach.
- **New measurement:** the loader products do not scale with the number of rows (core rank 8,443 on (8,143,44);
  8,444 with one group fewer, 8,445 with two fewer, 8,395 with 512 rows): one row is worth ≈ 1.9 Toffolis
  (−0.013%), not the 6.6 assumed in `chemistry.md` §2.3. Re-priced: R 8 → 7 −2.0% (was −7.5%), B 143 → 129 −1.6%
  (was −5.2%), R 8 → 6 −3.5%, brot 7 −5% to −6% (was −10%, and publicly null on accuracy). Only the C axis keeps its
  value (−0.53% per unit).
- Nulls: R/B reductions (not worth it after re-pricing), forcing the smaller QROAM block (scorer rule), a
  block-localized dictionary (tail-truncation errors far over the gate), twin vectors (net +0.6% to +2.3%); also
  closed: classical copies of the address (kickmix has no Z measurement), codebook columns (CHEM-N11), the
  address-permutation gauge (≤ −0.6% by a counting bound).
- Survivors: F3 non-bilinear loaders (ceiling −15%, realistic −1% to −3%, P 0.1, CPU), F6 rank-budgeted codes plus a
  C-axis retrain (−1% to −3%, P 0.2, P100), F7 more helper bits parked in the word registers (−0.2% to −0.6%).
  Best conceivable total ≈ −17% (loaders at their information floor + C 40), realistic January ceiling −2% to −5%.
- Joint reading of S2.18, S2.21 and S2.23: none of our paper studies finds a family at the leaders' scale (≈ −30%
  total cost); the leaders' path is not visible from public information.
- Not yet applied (left for the next session): the proposed IDEAS rows CHEM-B19..B22 and status changes, the eight
  null rows for `chemistry.md` §4 / NULLS.md, and the §2.3 correction (the report's last section lists them).
