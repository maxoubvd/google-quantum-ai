## What was done

- **Decoded the table format.** The generator's coefficient for the 12-bit root v (v = Q − 4096) is
  D(v) = A[a] + Σ_{i<L} (1 − 2v_i)·((S[a] >> (L − i)) + q_i), a = v >> L, C = 2D, where q_i is the circuit's
  rounding carry (`_coefficient_carry` in `candidate01/lifecycle.py`). L = 5 for the frozen 128-row table; the
  frozen model also derives the slope's lowest bit from the address ("implicit slope bit"), which only matters for
  the loaders. The formula reproduces all 4,096 frozen coefficients exactly.
- **`team/table_search.py`:** the generator's exact integer model (normalisation, integer root, seed, 10 coefficient
  rows, 27 signed-digit divider rows; a re-statement of Syed Asad Ali's `whole_model.py` parameterised by the
  table), the official targets and tolerances (`reference_values`), the margin `tol − |error|` in 2^-64 turns,
  physical-overflow detection, a per-row search (rows are independent under the base power-of-two angles) and the
  angle refit (active-set LP over 34 deltas, the method of Syed Asad Ali's `research/postgate_angle_refit/refit.py`).
- **Regression first:** on the frozen K = 7 model the tool gives minimum margin 31,966,138, exactly the recorded
  value, and its refit reaches 124,397,056 (Syed's refit: 124,370,688).
- **K = 6 search:** 64 rows, 13-bit intercept A, 7-bit slope S, 6 low bits; start from the K = 7 coefficients (row
  centre, average slope), coarse-to-fine coordinate search per row on 12 processes; then an exhaustive window
  (±48 on A, ±12 on S) on the 12 tightest rows.

## Why

INV-A02 (Ali Haydar Özdağ's idea, whose table is not public): a 64-row table halves the table loaders (public
result: 56 + 70 → 27 + 29 ANDs, −1.7% task cost). Its tools are not in the v12 source, so the table comes from our
own search.

## Results and analysis

| | Base angles | After angle refit |
|---|---|---|
| K = 7 (v12) | margin 31,966,138, 0 failures | 124,397,056 (ours), worst error/tolerance 0.932 |
| **K = 6 (ours)** | 1 failing input (row 45, −3,703,417); every other row ≥ 68.9M | **0 failures, margin 115,688,300, worst error/tolerance 0.920** |

- Row 45 is at its optimum within the 13/7-bit format (exhaustive window); the global refit repairs it with room
  to spare. The table words fit their registers: A in [2064, 8065] (13 bits), S ≤ 125 (7 bits).
- The refitted margin is 93% of v12's, so the rotation charge stays about the same (≈ +0.5 Toffoli-equivalent),
  far better than the public 64-row build (worst error/tolerance 0.99956, margin about 5× smaller).
- Run times: domain 1.7 s; K = 7 check 4.3 s (0.69 GB); K = 6 search 164 s on 12 processes; exhaustive window on
  12 rows 288 s; refit 6.4 s.
- `model_k6_v1.json` SHA-256 `eac13e49d2d41e83210c289fce859ce1e32cb0850d26ea77e2a1c8b167095981`.
