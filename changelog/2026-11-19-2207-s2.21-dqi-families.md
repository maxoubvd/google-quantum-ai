## Results and analysis

- Laws used for most verdicts: a per-position phase costs the multiplicative complexity of its Boolean function
  (ANDs uncomputed by measurement); an in-place map costs the same as its inverse (in-place multiplication modulo
  the locator costs what in-place division costs); any temporary 35-word polynomial (420 qubits) more than doubles
  the cost through Q²; one extra Toffoli per scan position ≈ +1.1%; the extractor needs ≈ 595 GF(2^24) products per
  direction at 1× width; the weight distribution forbids decoding fewer than 35 errors (2.77% of shots have
  weight 35).
- **Survivor: DQI-B13, convert-free Forney** (F3): the scan cell applies [w = 0]·Tr(a/b) computed from E, O and Ω
  (fits in the core, Q stays 890), deleting convert, unconvert and the projective finish (−122k static T). It breaks
  even only if Tr(a/b) over GF(2^12) costs ≲ 30 ANDs; the degree bound gives ≥ 11–12, the best construction found
  (through GF(64)/GF(8)) needs 69 (+42%). Ceiling −20% (2.75e11), P ≈ 0.03. Screen (S2.21b, ~8 h): minimum AND
  count of Tr(a/b) for n = 2–6 by SAT/resynthesis against the subfield construction; null if ≥ 75% of it.
- **Nulls on paper:** explicit convergents (+840 Q at slot 35), re-based query arithmetic (+82k / break-even at 2.8
  heads with +24 Q each / +110k), reciprocal convert (in-place multiply = divide), decode–re-encode (Hankel solve or
  +840 Q), 12-qubit carry bank (+118k; a lite variant joins DQI-A09), holding products across the mirror (≥ 267
  products per held word), quartic nodes (−1.6% best case against a +48k cubic precedent).
- Joint reading with S2.18: neither invsqrt nor dqi shows a paper family that reaches the leaders' scale; January's
  family effort should go to chemistry first (its shape moves were priced up to −7% to −10% in `chemistry.md`),
  with dqi on in-family steps (DQI-A01, A07/A17, A09 including the lite carry).
