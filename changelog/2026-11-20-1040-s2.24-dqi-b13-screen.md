## What was done

- `tools/mc_sat.py`: exact multiplicative complexity (minimum AND count of an XOR-AND graph) of a single- or
  multi-output Boolean function by SAT, one instance per AND budget k, budgets solved in parallel (fork) under a
  hard wall-clock limit. Encoding: operand selectors over inputs and earlier gates, Tseitin XOR chains per truth-table
  row, no operand constants (absorbable), last gate forced into a single output. Size O(2^n · k · (n + k)) clauses.
  Each SAT answer is decoded and re-evaluated on every row (`verified`).
- Validation on known values: 4-input AND = 3, majority of 3 = 1, x0x1 ⊕ x2x3 = 2: all exact.
- First run hung 56 min: the in-solver interrupt (`solve_limited` + timer) does not stop CaDiCaL from Python; the
  limit is now enforced by terminating the worker pool. Fixed and re-run.
- Screen: Tr(a/b) on GF(2^n)^2 (2n variables, b = 0 → 0), polynomial basis, for n = 2, 3, 4.

## Why

DQI-B13 (−20% on dqi if MC(Tr(a/b)) over GF(2^12) is about 12, break-even about 30, best construction 69) was the
only dqi family left by S2.21; this screen was the agreed next step (option B of 19 Nov).

## Results and analysis

| n | Variables | Exact MC | Tower construction | Ratio |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 2 | 4 | **2** (k = 1 UNSAT, k = 2 SAT) | 3 | 67% |
| 3 | 6 | **4 or 5** (k = 3 UNSAT in 31 s; k = 4 TIMEOUT at 3,000 s; k = 5 SAT in 28 s) | 6 | 67–83% |
| 4 | 8 | unknown (k = 5..11 all TIMEOUT at 3,000 s) | ≤ 11 | — |

- The decision rule of the report was: null if the best found at n = 4 and 6 is ≥ 75% of the tower; a second day only
  if ≤ 45%. Only n ≤ 3 is reachable, and it sits at 67–83%: extrapolated to n = 12 that gives ≈ 46–57 ANDs, well above
  the break-even of 30.4. Not a proof, so DQI-B13 is **PARKED (leans null)**, not NULL.
- Structural argument consistent with this: in the tower, the bilinear part T = a0·b1 + a1·b0 over GF(64) alone already
  costs about the break-even at n = 12 (not proven for non-tower circuits; the degree bound stays 11–12).
- Tool limits: exact SAT is practical up to 6 inputs (seconds to minutes) and fails at 8 inputs within 50 min, even
  for a budget the tower reaches (k = 11). Further progress would need symmetry breaking or a structured, tower-seeded
  resynthesis, several hours for an idea at P ≈ 0.03; not pursued.
- Consequence for CHEM-B19 (same question, multi-output): its exact small-case screen must stay at ≤ 6 inputs.
- Run times: n ≤ 3 runs 300 s on 6 cores (0.1 GB); n = 4 run 3,006 s on 8 cores (0.33 GB).
