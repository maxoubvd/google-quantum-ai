## What was done

- **Where the cutoff lives.** The v7 build (`integration_v7/build_v7.py`) goes through `v7_pipeline.emit` →
  `integration_v3/integrated_pipeline.emit_key_prefix` → the extractor adapter `first3_retained_norm_adapter`,
  which accepts any 3 ≤ j_min < 35. Only the two outer functions hard-code 15, and every source file is pinned by
  SHA-256 through several frozen manifests (v4, v5, v5b, v6, v7 component gates).
- **Team layer, no pinned file edited:** `src/team/build_team.py` runs `Pipeline.configure()` (all pin checks run
  unchanged), then replaces `integrated_pipeline.emit_key_prefix` at run time by a copy of its body that passes
  `--j-min` to the extractor (Syed Asad Ali's own method for his invsqrt patches). Three j_min-15 checks become
  recorded measurements: static T 384,003, the j_min-15 source ledger, and the frozen event inventory behind the
  exact expected-T snapshot (the first attempt failed on that inventory: 18–20 "tail_or_gauge_fresh_zero_phase"
  events instead of 21, a direct consequence of the cutoff). `team_build.sh` mirrors `build.sh` (same host pins).
- **Neutrality check:** at j_min 15 the team build is byte-identical to v12 (`7cbe8ecf…`).
- Built j_min 16, 17, 18 (≈ 95 s and 1.0 GB each).
- `tools/dqi_targeted.py`: draws shots with the official `sample_shots` but a one-hot weight distribution, and
  runs the official `verify(..., shots=...)` on identical inputs for every circuit.
- Gates: `tools/gate.py dqi --salts 16` on the four builds (kickmix-engine), and `--official` on j17.

## Why

DQI-A03 (P 0.95, a few hours): stop decoding error weights that the official distribution almost never draws,
in exchange for fewer Toffolis; judged on the expected cost, never on one draw.

## Results and analysis

| j_min | SHA-256 | Static T | Mean T (16 salts) | Failed shots / 16 draws | Cutoff failures per draw (exact) | Expected cost vs v12 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 15 (v12) | `7cbe8ecf` | 384,003 | 383,156.1 | 5 | 0.0005 | reference 3.6130e11 |
| 16 | `bec4d1a7` | 383,887 | 383,051.3 | 4 | 0.0030 | −0.026% |
| **17** | `cc0d7d25` | 383,771 | 382,946.2 | 7 | 0.0161 | **−0.048%** (sd per draw 0.264%) |
| 18 | `32b0a8b4` | 383,655 | 382,841.2 | 8 | 0.0790 | −0.048% (sd per draw 0.283%) |

- **Failure class, measured:** with 2,048 shots per weight (same inputs for all four), a decoder with cutoff j fails
  every shot of weight < j and no shot of weight ≥ j (weights 14–20 tested). The exact probability of weight < j
  under the official distribution (`optimal_dqi_weights²`) gives the cutoff failures above (weight ≤ 16: 1.575e-7
  per shot; ≤ 17: 7.713e-7).
- **v12's own failures are another class:** about 0.35 failed shots per draw (pooled over 64 draws) against 0.0005
  from its cutoff. Worth a later investigation (DQI-A07, rare-branch handlers).
- **Expected cost** = Q² × mean T × E[R(k)], k ~ Poisson(0.350 + cutoff rate), R from the official
  Clopper–Pearson bound. Cutoffs 17 and 18 tie; **17 kept** for its lower variance on the single private draw. The
  16-salt sample means (3.6124e11 for j17) are within noise of each other.
- **j17 official verifier, default salt: PASS, cost 360,535,516,958.68.** Its file is byte-identical to Ertuğrul
  Özer's public cutoff-17 file `cc0d7d25db72` (same cost as its published receipt): our generator now reproduces
  it from source.
- Gain: −0.048% task cost ≈ +0.016% score. Small, as the research expected (est. 0.05–0.08%).
- Run times: build 95 s; targeted test (4 circuits × 7 weights × 2,048 shots) ≈ 3 min; gate 16 salts ≈ 4.5 min
  per circuit (engine), official default salt 110 s.
