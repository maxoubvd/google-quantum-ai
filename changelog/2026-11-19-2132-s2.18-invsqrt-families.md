- F1: a parity row (subtract the odd divisor E when r ≥ 0, else add E + 1) costs one masked adder like a
  non-restoring row; its digit is the low bit of the next remainder, so the 12 rows without a dividend feed need no
  stored history: their digits can be measured out after the phase. Checked exhaustively for odd divisors 3..199
  plus 20,000 random hybrids. Lemma: a row with a one-bit dividend feed cannot be made history-free this way
  (confirms the N10 / NR-0261 nulls for feed rows).
- Conclusion: invsqrt from our own source is worth −3% to −9% at best (F1 plus in-family width moves); unless a family
  outside this list exists, the leaders' gap is more likely on dqi or chemistry. M2.C (dqi families) therefore moves
  up.
