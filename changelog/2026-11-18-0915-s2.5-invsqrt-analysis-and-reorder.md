## What was done

Started INV-A02 (Ali Haydar Özdağ's 64-row coefficient table) by reading the generator:

- Özdağ's build came from a **private** notebook (`ahoirg/qcc-invk6-k6c`); only the compiled file `4560ac0c` and a
  description are public (64 rows, 6 address bits, 13-bit intercept + 7-bit slope, loaders 27 + 29 ANDs instead
  of 56 + 70, slope bit S4 measured out and rebuilt with 2 ANDs, hold pass made robust to interleaved chains,
  angle refit).
- In Syed Asad Ali's v12 generator, the coefficient is C(v) = intercept(top 7 bits of the 12-bit root v) +
  slope × (low 5 bits), built by LOAD1 (13-bit D + 3-bit H) and the COEF stage (5 seven-bit adds). The table is
  the frozen output of a "phasefit" search, `research/recompute_root/models/phasefit_z1_K7_G4_m37_…json`
  (K = 7, 128 rows, A_bits 13, S_bits 6, low_bits 5, worst ratio 0.9488, minimum margin 31,966,138 units), pinned
  by SHA-256 in `common.py` and used by the integer model `whole_model.py` that the exact proof checks.
- **The search tools are not shipped**: no phasefit table search, no loader search (the loaders come from frozen
  certificate JSONs). An angle refit tool exists (`research/postgate_angle_refit/refit.py`). Five patch modules
  (`patch_donor_first60`, `patch_ld_first20_56b8`, `patch_second46_c19_70nb1`, `patch_w47_body`, `q46_loaders`)
  and the COEF stage assume 7 address bits / 128 rows.

So INV-A02 means: our own K = 6 table search certified on all inputs, our own loader synthesis, the generator
integration (COEF with 6 low bits, S4 trick, five patches), the angle refit and a regenerated proof model.
Re-estimated at 24–40 h instead of 16 h (priority roughly halved, still positive: −1.7% task cost, and the
prerequisite of INV-A03's −4.6%).

## Why

The plan's estimates are made before looking at the code; this one was wrong by a factor of 2. Following the
working method (split a step bigger than a day; order by priority), the user chose to do the two cheap dqi
ideas first and to split INV-A02 into four reviewed steps.
