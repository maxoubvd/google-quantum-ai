Problems found and fixed on the way:

- **Line endings.** Git on this machine has `core.autocrlf=true`, so the checked-out verifiers had
  CRLF endings and did not match the SHA-256 that the builders pin (the blobs in git are LF and do).
  Added `.gitattributes`: our text files LF, and `third_party/**`, the competition tree, `*.kmx` and
  `*.ipynb` never converted (`*.kmb`, `*.npz`, `*.xz` binary). Re-checked out the tracked files of the
  competition tree (no content change) so they are LF on disk; both verifiers now match their pins.
  Without this, a fresh clone would also have broken the builders' own `SHA256SUMS` checks.
- Commands passed through `wsl.exe -- bash -c '...'` lost their shell variables; multi-line WSL work now
  goes through script files.

Caveat for eligibility: the chemistry build copies a frozen `submission.npz` (trained weights) rather
than regenerating it; whether the hosts require the training to be reproducible too is an open forum
question.
