# S2.8 (first pass) — Loader synthesis for the 64-row table: bounds and a 44-AND construction

- **Date:** 2026-11-18 ~10:30 UTC (work), entry written 2026-11-19
- **Plan step:** S2.8 (IN PROGRESS), milestone M2.2
- **Files:** `generators/invsqrt/team/loader_synth.py` (created); `Analysis/PLAN.md` (S2.8 IN PROGRESS);
  `experiments/LEDGER.md` (E018)

## What was done

- Looked at the v12 loader certificates (`src/agents/loader2/certs/first_cap20_56b8.json`,
  `second_cap19_70nb1.json`): XOR-AND programs (`bank`, `CCX_new_coordinate`, `XOR_product_existing_coordinate`,
  `Clifford_frame_then_HMR`, `final_output_Clifford_frame` steps) that load **16 bits** (13-bit intercept A + 3
  slope bits) from the 7-bit address in 56 and 70 ANDs under work caps of 20 and 19 qubits; the other slope bits
  come from an address-derived "implicit" bit and a 21-Toffoli root-value decoder outside the loaders.
- `team/loader_synth.py`: bilinear-split synthesis. The 6 address bits are split into two groups; all monomials
  of each group are computed (the monomials of degree ≥ 2 cost one AND each); every output bit is then a bilinear
  form over the two monomial vectors, and products of XORs of monomials are needed for the non-free part.
  Computes the GF(2) rank lower bound and tries a randomized greedy.
- A row-wise construction (for each non-constant monomial of one group, as many products as the rank of the
  corresponding rows over all outputs) was evaluated for every split.

## Why

INV-A02 needs loaders for our 64-row table (A 13 bits, S 7 bits from 6 address bits). Ali Haydar Özdağ reached
27 + 29 ANDs with a private search tool; v12's tools are not shipped.

## Results and analysis

- Lower bound: at least 19–20 products beyond the monomial ANDs, for every split.
- Row-wise construction: **44 ANDs per loader for all 20 bits** (best split: one address bit against the other
  five: 26 monomial ANDs + 18 products). For comparison, v12 spends 56 + 70 ANDs for 16 bits (plus the decoder).
- The rank-greedy finds nothing: a rank-1 product almost never lies in the span of the remaining targets on its
  own, so a useful search must combine several products (common-subexpression search, annealing or SAT). That is
  left for later.
- Run time: bounds for all 41 splits in ~100 s (pure Python; the greedy dominates).

User decision (2026-11-18): integrate first (S2.9) with the 44-AND construction, to get a complete invsqrt
candidate earlier, and optimise the loaders afterwards.
