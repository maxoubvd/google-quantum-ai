## What was done

- **S2.1 (GEN-A01).** Downloaded kickmix-engine 0.6.0 (manwithacat, Rust, Apache-2.0) from its GitHub release;
  wheel SHA-256 `76f02cce…d178` matches the release's `SHA256SUMS.txt`; only dependency numpy. `doctor`: AVX2
  kernel selected (no AVX-512 on this CPU). Ran `verify --cross-check` (engine and official verifier, every
  field compared) on our three v12 circuits; then engine-only runs to measure time and memory.
- **S2.2 (GEN-A02).** `tools/kmb.py`: memory-mapped struct-of-arrays reader for `.kmb` v2 with header and
  array-length validation; counts; static Toffoli-likes; expected Toffolis per shot by bit-parallel replay of
  the classical trajectory (HMR coins, BIT_* writes, condition stack; 64 shots per uint64 word, 2^20 shots by
  default); allocated-width profile; per-stage tables when the circuit has INFO_TAGs. Layout learned from
  GrantPrater's `vqcc_ir.py` (Apache-2.0), read as documentation; the code is ours.

## Why

M2.1: a faster try-and-verify loop multiplies every later milestone. The engine is used only for iteration;
the official verifiers remain the gate (CLAUDE.md).

## Results and analysis

Engine vs official verifier (WSL, 12 cores):

| Task | Agreement | Engine | Official | Speed-up |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| dqi, default salt (cross-check) | identical, 0 errors | 19.6 s | 118.8 s (2 workers) | 6.1× |
| dqi, private-1 (cross-check) | identical, **2 value errors reproduced** | 18.2 s | 115.2 s | 6.3× |
| dqi, private-2 / private-3 (engine only) | 1 / 0 errors, same costs as S1.5 | 15.7 / 17.7 s, **0.55 GB** | (4.7 GB) | — |
| invsqrt, full 64 shots × 3,133,452 (cross-check) | identical; cost 80,162.732, worst 0.973576 | 15.2 s | 183.3 s | 12.1× |
| invsqrt, reduced `--batch-size 12` | not supported: "the engine path supports batch_size=256 only" | — | 26 s | — |
| dfthcsos (cross-check) | identical, 296,635,967.59 | 43.9 s | 42.1 s | 1.0× |

Correction: the full official invsqrt run takes **3 min** here, not "about 1 h" as `CLAUDE.md` said (that
figure was never measured on this machine). In S1.5 the full run was stopped after 2 minutes on that wrong
estimate; it has now been done (above), which completes the S1.5 verification of the baseline:
invsqrt full-run cost 80,162.732 (vs 80,162.673 reduced).

`tools/kmb.py` on the baselines:

| Circuit | Result | Reference | Time |
|---|---|---|---:|
| invsqrt v12 | Q 46, static 1,599, mean 1,511.580 ± 0.008 (2^20 shots), allocated-width peak 46 | verifier mean 1,511.570 (200M shots): 1.3 σ | 0.24 s |
| chemistry, 15 loaders | 4,074.0 Toffolis per pass, × 2 = 8,148; max 128 qubits | scorer ANGLE term 8,148 | 0.1 s |
| dqi v12 | Q 889, bits 53,911, 52.9M ops, static 384,003, 71,531 HMR | verifier `max_toffoli_count` 384,003, Q 889 | 0.85 s |

Notes: the v12 circuits carry no INFO_TAGs, so per-stage tables need the generator's own stage map. The dqi
expected count is not computed by `kmb.py` because its classical inputs follow the verifier's distribution;
the engine gives it exactly in 16 s.
